I am a PhD candidate in the [Tyrrell Lab](https://sites.uab.edu/tyrrelllab/) in the Department of
Pathology at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, supported by an American Heart Association
Predoctoral Fellowship. I study how the aging immune system contributes to vascular cognitive
impairment and dementia.

My research focuses on age-associated CD8+ T cells, a cytotoxic, Granzyme K–expressing population
that accumulates with age, and on how their interactions with reactive glia shape the neuroimmune
environment of the aging brain. I combine rodent models of neurovascular disease, brain immune-cell
isolation, spectral flow cytometry, and single-cell and spatial analysis.

## Research interests

- Age-associated CD8+ T cells in brain aging and dementia
- Neuroimmune interactions between T cells and microglia
- Vascular contributions to cognitive impairment and dementia (VCID)
- High-dimensional immune profiling: spectral flow cytometry, single-cell RNA-seq, and spatial proteomics
- Reproducible computational pipelines for cytometry data

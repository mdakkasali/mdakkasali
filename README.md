PhD candidate in the [Tyrrell Lab](https://sites.uab.edu/tyrrelllab/), Department of Pathology,
University of Alabama at Birmingham, and an American Heart Association Predoctoral Fellow.
I study how the aging immune system contributes to vascular cognitive impairment and dementia.

My research focuses on age-associated CD8+ T cells, cytotoxic and Granzyme K–expressing
populations that accumulate with age, and on how their interactions with reactive glia shape
the neuroimmune environment of the aging brain. My work combines rodent models of
neurovascular disease, brain immune-cell isolation, spectral flow cytometry, and single-cell
and spatial analysis.

## Research areas

- Age-associated CD8+ T cells in brain aging and dementia
- Neuroimmune interactions between T cells and microglia
- Vascular contributions to cognitive impairment and dementia (VCID)
- High-dimensional immune profiling: spectral flow cytometry, single-cell RNA-seq, and spatial proteomics
- Reproducible computational pipelines for cytometry data

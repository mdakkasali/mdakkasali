PhD candidate in the [Tyrrell Lab](https://sites.uab.edu/tyrrelllab/), Department of Pathology,
University of Alabama at Birmingham. I study how the aging immune system contributes to
vascular cognitive impairment and dementia.

My work centres on **age-associated CD8+ T cells** — cytotoxic and Granzyme K–expressing
populations that accumulate with age — and how their interactions with reactive glia shape the
neuroimmune environment of the aging brain. I work across the bench and the terminal:
rodent models of neurovascular disease, brain immune-cell isolation, spectral flow cytometry,
and single-cell and spatial analysis.

Supported by a **2026 American Heart Association Predoctoral Fellowship**.

## Research areas

- Age-associated CD8+ T cells in brain aging and dementia
- Neuroimmune interactions between T cells and microglia
- Vascular contributions to cognitive impairment and dementia (VCID)
- High-dimensional immune profiling — spectral flow cytometry, single-cell RNA-seq, spatial proteomics
- Reproducible computational pipelines for cytometry data
